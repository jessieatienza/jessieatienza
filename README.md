## Hi, I'm Jessie 🤗
 
I'm a Product Leader who builds.
 
Sometimes that means strategy, discovery, positioning, roadmaps, metrics, organizational alignment, and all the other things you'd expect from a product leader. Sometimes it means mentorship and coaching, other times it means establishing a product practice in a place that doesn't have one yet. Sometimes it means opening an editor and building the thing I want to see myself. I love all of it.
 
## I like breaking things down to First Principles
 
A surprising amount of modern software is built by taking an old idea and making it progressively better or pivoting by a fraction. Better dashboards. Smarter notifications. More intelligent search. Automated workflows. AI copilots. I'm fascinated by a different question: **> What if the thing we're improving isn't the thing we'd even invent today?** 

That's where I like to work. Dig until I find the root of the problem, then strip the product that solves it all the way back. I like figuring out what people are actually trying to accomplish and their desired outcomes and pursuing that relentlessly. Question the inherited abstraction>Build an alternative> See where it breaks>Learn something>Do it again

I use coding tools as a way to validate my product ideas because nowadays, the fastest way to discover whether a product idea is good is to make it exist.
